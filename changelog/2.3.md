# Changelog: 2.3

## [2.3.21] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- Mods that need Immersive Portals 6.0.7 or later refused to load alongside this mod, which reported its own version (2.3.x) for Immersive Portals. The Immersive Portals mod ids (`immersive_portals_core`, `immersive_portals`, `imm_ptl`, `q_misc_util`) now report the upstream version this mod is built on, with ours as a suffix (`6.0.7+ipa.2.3.21`).

### Added
- A mod id of our own, `ip_aeronautics`, reporting this mod's version: the one for other mods to check when they look for Immersive Portals: Aeronautics itself. Launchers and the mod list show the jar under it.

### Changed
- Distant Horizons LODs through nearby non-full blocks, in your own view and in portal views, are verified in game (2.3.20).

## [2.3.20] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- With Distant Horizons, fences, stairs, slabs and other non-full blocks seen through a portal still turned into LODs against the sky too close (2.3.19's estimate of where the other side's terrain ends came out at about 33 blocks). Portal views no longer use a distance at all: LODs are left out exactly in the chunks the portal view draws (loaded, with their neighbours, for long enough to be built, and within render distance), and fill in everywhere else. With an Iris shader pack the shader pack draws the LODs, and 2.3.19's distance is still used.

## [2.3.19] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- With Distant Horizons, looking through a portal, fences, stairs, slabs and other non-full blocks on the other side still turned into LODs from 16 blocks away wherever the sky was behind them (2.3.18 fixed only your own view). LODs in a portal view now start where the other side's drawn terrain ends, worked out from the chunks actually loaded around the far portal and from how far you stand from the portal, or where DH would start them in your own view if that is closer. No band of empty sky between the terrain and the LODs.

## [2.3.18] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- With Distant Horizons, fences, stairs, slabs and other non-full blocks turned into LODs from 16 blocks away wherever the sky was behind them (with no portal anywhere near): DH's far terrain showed through their gaps. Inherited from Immersive Aeronautics, which made DH start its LODs 16 blocks from the camera everywhere instead of near the edge of the render distance. The player's own view now keeps DH's own setting; LODs seen through a portal still start close, so they fill in where the other side's loaded chunks end.

## [2.3.17] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- With Create Dynamic Lights, the lamps of a ship straddling a portal lit only the ship's own side: lamps on the part already through lit nothing on the other side (and lit the ground behind the portal on your side instead), and seen from the other side the ship had no light at all until it was fully through. Lamps past the portal now light the other side, lamps before it light their own, from either side.

### Changed
- Ship lamps with Create Dynamic Lights are verified in game (2.3.16). 2.3.15's diagnostics are removed.

## [2.3.16] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- With Create Dynamic Lights, lamps on Sable ships lit only the ship, not the world or other ships around them (also with no portal anywhere near). The client copy of every ship had no chunks registered with it: Sable records them as the server sends them, through the vanilla client chunk cache, and Immersive Portals replaces that cache with its own, so they only went into IP's. Ships still looked right (their blocks are read through the world), but anything that walks a ship's chunks found nothing: Create Dynamic Lights found no lamps. Ship chunks are now registered with their ship again, as without IP (2.3.15's diagnostics: 4 ships, 0 chunks). The diagnostics stay in this version.

## [2.3.15] - 2026-10-17

### Changed
- Temporary diagnostics for ship lamps not lighting their surroundings with Create Dynamic Lights (still seen with 2.3.14, with no portal in view): every 5 s for the first 5 minutes, `[IPDynLightsDiag]` log lines count each stage of a dynamic light, from the ship's lamps Sable holds to the vertex light Sodium meshes the world with.

## [2.3.14] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- With Create Dynamic Lights (SodiumDynamicLights), lamps on Sable ships did not light the world or other ships around them. SodiumDynamicLights updates its lights once every 50 ms against whichever world renderer draws first, and with a portal in view that was often the portal's other dimension: the light's surroundings were redrawn there instead of in your world, and the light counted as done, so a lamp on a ship that was not moving never lit anything. Each dimension now updates only its own lights; lights in a dimension seen through a portal light that view too.

## [2.3.13] - 2026-10-17

### Changed
- Driving a train through a portal is verified in game (2.3.12), standing by it and riding it. The portal-track gold glow is verified too (2.3.10).

### Fixed
- Mod developers' dev runs (`runClient`/`runServer` with this mod as a dependency) crashed at bootstrap with a `NullPointerException` from `ModList.get()` in `ServerLevel`'s class initialiser (a mod developer's report). A dev run checks the vanilla commands during bootstrap, which loads `ServerLevel` before mods are loaded; two of our mixins read the mod list there. Players were never affected.
- The mod did not start without Sable (it crashed at boot, also on a dedicated server), nor without Create: our Sable and Create compat mixins were applied to vanilla classes even when those mods were absent. They are now skipped without their mod. Found while reproducing the report above.

## [2.3.12] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- Driving a train through a portal while standing by it (not on it) still stopped the controls in 2.3.11. 2.3.11's log showed why: when the carriage had passed through, Create removed its copy on your side, and removing it stops the train controls, telling you since you were in that dimension. Create also only lets you drive within reach of the controls (your block reach plus 8), measured in one dimension. Now:
  - removing a copy while the carriage's other copy lives keeps you driving that copy;
  - your reach to controls past a portal is measured through the portal next to you, so you can drive the train on until its controls are out of reach on the far side, as you could without the portal;
  - your inputs find the copy you steer even when it is in another dimension;
  - when it does go out of reach through the portal, you are told (Create only told players in the train's dimension, which would have left you controlling with your movement keys taken).

## [2.3.11] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- Driving a train through a portal still stopped the controls in 2.3.10 ("Stopped Controlling Contraption"). 2.3.10 only switched the controls over once you were in another dimension than the carriage you were driving. Create also stops as soon as that carriage's copy is removed, which can happen before you cross (it discards the copy a few ticks after the carriage has left, while a driver standing at the back is still on this side). The controls now also follow when the copy is removed, and wait up to 5 seconds for a copy on your side while the train exists.

### Added
- Diagnostics for drivers losing the controls at a portal: every stop is logged with its cause (client: `[IPTrainControls] client: Create stops controlling ...`, server: `... stops being controlled by ...`), as well as the controls following a carriage or waiting for its copy.

## [2.3.10] - 2026-10-17

### Changed
- Create's portal tracks no longer glow purple into every portal. The glow now follows the portal's frame: purple for obsidian (nether portals, as before), gold for glowstone (the Aether's), plain rails for any other frame or none (portals made with the portal wand, other mods' portals). End portals keep Create's purple. The gold is recoloured from your installed Create's own texture when the game loads its textures; the mod ships no copy of Create's art.

### Fixed
- Driving a Create train through a portal no longer drops you from the controls. Create kept your controls bound to the carriage on the side you started from and stopped once you were on the other side (stock Create does the same at its own portals). The controls now move to the carriage's copy on your side, held keys and all, so the train keeps going without a hitch. Logged (`[IPTrainControls] ... keeps the controls`, client: `controls follow carriage`).

## [2.3.9] - 2026-10-17

### Changed
- The flash of a carriage's front ahead of a train entering a portal is verified fixed in game (2.3.8). The `[IPTrainTrace]` frame trace is now off unless `-Dip_aeronautics.debug.trainTrace=true`.

## [2.3.8] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- Create trains entering a portal: the copy of the carriage's front that flashed ahead of the train as its front wheels reached the portal. 2.3.7's trace caught it: the carriage's far-side copy, created by the server at its own position (ahead of your client's train, more so the faster it goes), was drawn uncut for its first frame. Flywheel builds a new carriage's model on its worker threads while the frame is being prepared, and IP worked out which parts to cut or hide before those threads had finished, so the new copy was missed for that one frame. IP now waits until they have. The `[IPTrainTrace]` frame trace stays on in this test version.

## [2.3.7] - 2026-10-17

### Added
- Diagnostics for the copy of a carriage's front that still flashes ahead of a train entering a portal (2.3.6 did not fix it): while a carriage crosses a portal, every frame is logged (`[IPTrainTrace]`): each of its copies' state and position, where it was drawn and how in each pass. `-Dip_aeronautics.debug.trainTrace=false` turns it off.

## [2.3.6] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- Create trains entering a portal: the brief copy of the carriage's front ahead of the train, seen through the portal as the front wheels reach it (further ahead the faster the train). The server creates the carriage's far-side copy at that moment, at its own position, which runs ahead of your client's train; that copy is kept hidden until your client places it, but its very first frame was drawn anyway, uncut, because the hiding was worked out only for Flywheel parts drawn at least once before. New Flywheel parts are now cut or hidden from their first frame, and a carriage copy your client has not placed yet is never drawn. 2.3.5 did not fix this (its case never came up).

## [2.3.5] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- Create trains entering a portal: the front of a carriage no longer flashes ahead of the train, seen through the portal, as its front wheels reach it. From a carriage's second trip through a portal on, its copy on the far side started out about half a block to a block too far on for a few ticks (it skipped the adjustment that lines the two sides up). Logged in `debug.log` each time this would have happened (`[IPTrainCrossing] ... wheels arrived`).

## [2.3.4] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- The game crashed loading a world with Petrolpark's Library installed. Petrolpark makes one of the game's internal chunk methods public, and IP replaces that method with its own private version, which Mixin refused. IP's replacements now adopt whatever visibility the game's method has, so other mods that widen methods this way no longer clash with them. Verified in game.

## [2.3.3] - 2026-10-17

### Fixed
- With Netherstorm, lighting an obsidian frame with flint and steel made a vanilla nether portal instead of an immersive one. Netherstorm lights frames itself as soon as the frame is clicked, before the fire that IP listens to is placed. Any mod that fills a frame with vanilla portal blocks this way now gets IP's portal instead (unless IP's nether portal mode is "vanilla"). IP also accepts the frame blocks other mods add to nether portals, so Netherstorm's polished obsidian blocks make immersive portals, and those portals stay up instead of breaking.
- With Halcyon (or another mod using Databank's screen effects), its effects no longer run inside portal views, where they could wipe the screen around a visible portal while active. Effects that place things in the world no longer use a portal's camera after a portal was drawn.
- Both verified in game.

## [2.3.2] - 2026-10-16

### Fixed
- Without Sodium, blocks placed on a Sable ship often stayed invisible and broken ones stayed drawn until F3+A. IP replaces the game's table of terrain sections with its own, and that bypassed the hook through which Sable learns that a ship's block changed; such changes now reach the ship. With Sodium this already worked. Verified by the player who reported it.

## [2.3.1] - 2026-10-16

First public release since 2.2.4. Groups the invisible rims for frameless portals (2.3.0, details below) with a fix for Distant Horizons on Linux.

### Fixed
- With Distant Horizons on Linux with Mesa drivers (AMD and others), the other dimension's terrain and LODs were drawn outside a portal's frame, across the screen wherever they were nearer than the world behind them; worse after the first trip through the portal. DH saves and restores the stencil settings while its own framebuffer is bound, and Mesa reports the portal's stencil reference as 0 there, so DH restored 0 and the portal view lost its outline. IP's real settings are now recorded at the start of each DH render call in a portal view, given to every state snapshot DH takes during the call, and written back after it. Found and verified with three players' test builds (2.2.5-2.2.7, built on 2.2.4).

## [2.3.0] - 2026-10-15

### Fixed
- Ships straddling a portal without a frame (made by command) slid out of the opening at its edges, the part past the portal landing back behind it, and a ship wider than the opening went through it whole. Such portals now have an invisible frame around the opening that only ships feel (a quarter of a block wide, as thick as a nether portal's frame): a straddling ship rests against the edge, and a ship wider than the opening lies on it like on a frame. Portals with a frame of blocks, or set into a floor or wall, get none.
