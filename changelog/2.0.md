# Changelog: 2.0

## [2.0.8] - 2026-10-14

### Fixed
- A ship slammed into a portal's frame hard enough to end up with frame blocks inside its hull (reported on 2.0.7: an airship driven across the edge of a large nether portal, the frame's edge pillar through its envelope) is no longer stuck for good. Once it has been a quarter of a block or more inside the frame for half a second, it is let through those frame blocks, on both sides of the portal, until it is clear of them: it can be flown or dragged out (or floats or falls free), and the frame is solid for it again as soon as it is out.

## [2.0.7] - 2026-10-14

### Fixed
- A ship hitting a portal's frame (pressed against the obsidian with the physics staff, a fast impact) no longer starts "straddling": it is no longer cut at the portal plane with a sliver drawn in the other dimension. A ship wider than the opening, flying at the frame with its middle headed into the opening, had its crossing opened one tick early, and resting against the frame (exactly the half block before the plane that kept a crossing open) kept it for good; meanwhile the frame's inner half past the plane did not collide with it, so a hard push could sink it into the frame. The early opening now skips a ship about to hit the frame, a crossing closes once the ship rests against the frame with nothing at the plane, and a ship pushed into the frame with nothing really through the opening neither starts nor keeps a crossing.
- A ship going through a portal no longer bumps into things it cannot touch: while crossing, the parts of a ship wider than the opening collided with whatever was behind the portal it was entering (terrain behind the far portal, the far portal's frame from behind), which could hold it up, shove it, or wedge it in the frame half in each dimension. Only the part past the portal plane collides in the other dimension now.

## [2.0.6] - 2026-10-14

### Fixed
- A fast ship or block no longer falls out of portals placed close together (stacked nether portals, a portal leading a few blocks above itself). Once it was fast enough to come out of one portal already touching the next one's plane, that portal never saw it enter and it fell through. A ship's crossing history now carries its incoming motion through the hand-off (cut back to where it was clear of the portal it came out of), so the next portal sees the entry at any speed.

### Removed
- Physics Mod's ocean handling, inherited from the fork. Physics Mod turns its ocean, snow and vine physics off by itself next to Immersive Portals; only skipping its draw in portal views stays.
- The first-person hand diagnostics of 2.0.2 and 2.0.4 (`[IPHand]` log lines, per-frame GL state reads) and 2.0.5's Veil first-person framebuffer rebuild, which did not fix the invisible hand after a shader pack toggle (deferred, `bugs.md` open item 4).

## [2.0.5] - 2026-10-14

### Fixed
- The first-person arm and held item no longer disappear (until restart) after turning a shader pack on and off in game. Veil, bundled in Sable, draws the hand into a framebuffer that wraps the main framebuffer's colour texture and kept the old texture after Immersive Portals recreated the main framebuffer when switching portal renderers; it is now rebuilt when that texture changed.

## [2.0.4] - 2026-10-14

### Added
- First-person hand watch, part 2. 2.0.3's log showed the hands drawn every frame (`drewHandsLastFrame=true`) while invisible after turning a shader pack off. `[IPHand] at hand draw:` lines now record, whenever it changes, the GL state at the hand draw outside portal passes (framebuffers, viewport, depth, stencil, color mask, scissor, clip distances, IP's stencil settings), how many arms were drawn and whether any of their fragments passed the depth and stencil tests (occlusion query).

## [2.0.3] - 2026-10-14

### Fixed
- Crash (`StackOverflowError` in `IPStraddleHandoffLatch.followedPose`) when a ship went through a portal into its own dimension (a custom Overworld-to-Overworld portal), also on every reload of a world with a ship in such a portal. The client's hand-off latch looked for the ship's new copy in the destination level, which was the ship's own level, and linked the old copy to itself. A ship crossing within one dimension is no longer held for the hand-off (Sable keeps one ship per UUID in a level, so the two copies cannot coexist): it blinks out until its new copy's meshes compile.

## [2.0.2] - 2026-10-14

### Added
- First-person hand watch (reported on 2.0.1: arm and held item gone until restart after turning a shader pack off in game). `[IPHand]` lines in `latest.log` record, whenever it changes, the state deciding whether vanilla draws the hands (`renderHand`, panorama, camera type, F1, spectator, sleeping, shader pack, portal renderer) and whether it drew them in the previous frame. If `GameRenderer.renderHand` is found off at the start of a frame (only vanilla's panorama screenshots do that), it is turned back on and logged.

## [2.0.1] - 2026-10-14

### Fixed
- Iris shader packs: the far half of a ship straddling a portal was not drawn, and the ship was not cut at the portal in its own dimension. A pack's terrain programs (`terrain_solid`, `terrain_cutout`, `terrain_translucent`, `moving_block`) now carry the straddle cut and IP's portal clip plane like the vanilla terrain shaders. Shadows are not cut (a pack's shadow programs are unchanged).
- Distant Horizons no longer shows "Immersive Portals Mixin Fail" in chat after a long frame (Iris compiling a shader pack for a dimension seen through a portal takes several seconds); a missing injection still warns.
- No more "The current shaderpack is incompatible with Immersive Portals" warning for Complementary: upstream Immersive Portals' online mod info (its incompatible mods and shader packs, its latest version) is no longer fetched.

## [2.0.0] - 2026-10-13

First public release: ships straddle portals (on by default); Create trains, the physics staff, ropes, balloons and Shield Generators work across them. No gameplay changes since 1.24.16.

### Changed
- Licence: this project's own code (new files, its changes to Immersive Portals and Immersive Aeronautics code, its changes to the Sable copy) is now under the PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0 instead of Apache-2.0; the code taken from Immersive Portals, Immersive Aeronautics, IPSable and Rapier stays Apache-2.0. The jar ships `META-INF/LICENSE` (PolyForm Shield, also covering the Sable copy, so `LICENSE-SABLE.md` is gone), `META-INF/LICENSE-APACHE` and `META-INF/NOTICE` (with a `Required Notice:` line); every source file header says which applies.
- The jar lists the Rust crates linked into the `ipa_rapier` native libraries with their licences (`natives/ipa_rapier/THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt`, written by `scripts/native-licenses.py`).
- Quieter `latest.log`: no "@Mixin target ... was not found" warnings at start-up when Distant Horizons, Iris, Physics Mod or Shield Generators are not installed (those mixins are skipped instead), and the train-crossing model diagnostics and the per-ship "synced through the portal" lines on player crossings log at debug.
