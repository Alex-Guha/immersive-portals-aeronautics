# Changelog: 2.1

## [2.1.0] - 2026-10-14

Groups the fixes of the test builds since 2.0.0 (2.0.1-2.0.8, details in their entries in [2.0.md](2.0.md)). No changes since 2.0.8.

### Fixed
- Shader packs (Iris): a ship going through a portal is cut at the portal and its far half drawn with a pack on (verified with Complementary and BSL). No more false "The current shaderpack is incompatible with Immersive Portals" warning (Complementary), and no "Immersive Portals Mixin Fail" in chat with Distant Horizons after a long pack compile (2.0.1).
- Crash (`StackOverflowError`) when a ship went through a portal into its own dimension (a custom Overworld-to-Overworld portal), also on every reload of a world with a ship in such a portal (2.0.3).
- Ships and portal edges: hitting a portal's frame no longer starts a crossing (a ship cut at the portal with a sliver drawn in the other dimension); while crossing, the parts of a ship wider than the opening no longer bump into terrain behind the far portal (2.0.7); a ship slammed into a frame hard enough to end up with frame blocks inside its hull is let through those blocks until it is clear instead of being stuck for good (2.0.8).
- A fast ship no longer falls out of portals placed close together (stacked portals, a portal leading a few blocks above itself) (2.0.6).

### Known issues
- The first-person arm and held item can disappear after turning a shader pack on and off in game; a restart brings them back.
