# Changelog: 2.5

## [2.5.13] - 2026-10-24

### Fixed
- With a shader pack, portal views showed block entities (chests, Create's cogs, cranks and pulleys, docking connectors), entities and particles that stand behind the far portal, on the side the view looks out from: the pack's programs for them had no portal clip plane, only its terrain programs did. They now get it, as the vanilla shaders do without a pack.

## [2.5.12] - 2026-10-23

### Fixed
- Cobbleloots works alongside: chunks no longer fail to load on the client, which left them unlit or missing, or ended the session with a network protocol error (a ClassCastException in Cobbleloots). Our client told other mods that each chunk it received was newly generated, which only ever happens on the server.

## [2.5.11] - 2026-10-23

### Fixed
- Create: Pocket Sized works alongside: scaled ships now collide with the world and with other ships at their scaled size instead of at 1x (bigger ships no longer sink into blocks, smaller ones no longer float). Pocket's library (Pym) changes Sable's own physics backend, which we replace for ships straddling portals, so with Pocket installed Sable's own physics runs. Ships then cross portals in one step, as without our physics backend, instead of passing through them gradually.

## [2.5.10] - 2026-10-22

### Fixed
- Dimensions that another mod adds while the game runs (Infinite Dimensions) no longer crash or kick with "Missing Dimension": the server gives them an id and tells every player, and the client accepts them as server dimensions. Before, they only worked after reloading the world.
- The game no longer crashes ("The validated expression is false") a moment after a vanilla-style dimension change (vanilla nether portals, Infinite Dimensions' portals, respawning in another dimension): the client-side scans for Create pipe stubs, track stubs and shaft halves, through-portal ship ticking and the straddle hand-over waited for Immersive Portals to list the client's worlds again, instead of reading the list while it was empty.

## [2.5.9] - 2026-10-22

### Added
- A config screen, opened with the Config button of the mod's entry in the Mods list. It has the options the old screen had, in a Client and a World section, and saves to `config/immersive_portals.json` when you press Done. Every option takes effect at once except two, which take effect the next time you load or join a world: they are marked with a gold *, and changing one while in a world shows a reminder to rejoin.

### Changed
- Cloth Config is no longer needed. The config file stays as it was: existing settings carry over unchanged. A config file that cannot be read is kept as `immersive_portals.json.broken` before the defaults replace it.
- The Mods list shows one entry for this mod instead of five. The Immersive Portals mod ids are still declared, so mods that look for or require Immersive Portals work as before.

### Removed
- Physics Mod's ocean handling, inherited from the fork. Physics Mod turns its ocean, snow and vine physics off by itself next to Immersive Portals; only skipping its draw in portal views stays.
- The `/imm_ptl_client_debug config` command, which opened the old config screen.

## [2.5.8] - 2026-10-22

### Added
- Create trains through portals that lead back into the same dimension: Create Teleporters' portal pairs, framed command portals, any plain IP portal with portal blocks. Lay a straight track up to each end as for nether portals; trains cross both ways, riders are handed over, and a train can stand with carriages on both sides. The crossing looks as at nether portals: carriages cut exactly at the portal plane, no purple glass, the one-block jump at the portal closed over the track, seated riders carried through with their view.
- Driving a train through such a portal keeps the controls, riding or standing by within reach through the portal.
- A train left standing in such a portal stays intact through a save and reload.

### Fixed
- A bogey crossing a portal that turns the track (into another dimension or not) no longer shows turned by the portal's rotation on one side, nor turns for a moment as the carriage's last axle leaves the portal.

### Changed
- Trains through Create Teleporters' portals within one dimension no longer go through Create Teleporters' own train code. Track pairs laid at such portals with 2.5.3-2.5.7 keep using it while Create Teleporters is installed; break one end and lay it again to switch.
- Immersive Portals never moves a Create carriage through a portal by itself; Create does that.

## [2.5.7] - 2026-10-21

### Fixed
- Opening Create's config screen (or any other screen from Create's UI library, Catnip) no longer makes most of the world invisible behind it, leaving only water, glass, clouds and portal views. Catnip's stencil masks left the GPU's stencil rule at "draw only where the stencil is 1", and IP drew the outer world with that rule until its portal pass; IP now sets the stencil rule itself at the start of each frame. The same fixes any other mod that leaves a stencil rule behind, which could keep the world hidden after the screen closed.

## [2.5.6] - 2026-10-21

### Changed
- A track at a portal shows the whole bridge through the portal, both halves, as soon as either end has a track: the far portal block draws the other half of the rails (up to its face) when the other end has no track yet, as pipes do. Display only; trains still stop at the portal until both ends have a track.

## [2.5.5] - 2026-10-21

### Changed
- A track laid up to a portal gets its piece into the portal at once, whether or not the other end has a track yet (as pipes and shafts do); trains stop there until the other end is laid, then the two connect. Breaking one end leaves the other with its piece into the portal; the portal going away turns both back into ordinary tracks. No chat message any more.
- Tracks laid up to a portal before it was lit or opened connect once it opens (they used to need placing again).

## [2.5.4] - 2026-10-21

### Changed
- Create train tracks through IP portals (nether portals, Create Teleporters' and any other framed portal) are no longer placed for you at the other end. Lay an ordinary straight track up to each end of the portal; once both are there, they connect and only the piece reaching into the portal is added (players cannot place that one). Until then the track stays an ordinary track and players nearby are told where the other end's track goes. Breaking one end, or the portal going away, turns the other end back into the ordinary track it was (curves kept) instead of destroying it. Pairs connected by earlier versions keep the old behaviour.

### Fixed
- The other end of a portal track is where the portal carries the train out, following the portal's rotation. Create Teleporters' portals turn a train around (in at one frame's front, out at the other frame's front), and the track used to be placed on the wrong face of the other frame. Tracks connected by 2.5.2-2.5.3 at those portals are on the wrong side: break them and lay both ends again.

## [2.5.3] - 2026-10-21

### Added
- Create trains through a Create Teleporters portal pair within one dimension (with its "Immersive Portals Compatibility" option on). Create only joins portal tracks across dimensions; Create Teleporters' own train code gives each side of a same-dimension portal a key of its own, and a portal track laid against one of its immersive portals is now tagged the way Create Teleporters tags its own, so that code takes the train across. Same-dimension portals not made by Create Teleporters still get no train track. The crossing looks as it does in Create Teleporters without Immersive Portals (Create's own cut at the portal); the seamless look of trains through nether portals is not there yet.

## [2.5.2] - 2026-10-21

### Added
- Create train tracks, shafts and pipes now bridge portals that stand in a frame but have no portal blocks of their own, such as Create Teleporters' immersive portals (with its "Immersive Portals Compatibility" option on): the opening gets IP's invisible portal blocks, as a nether portal has, and loses them when the portal goes away. Applies to any plain portal that is upright or flat along the block grid, unscaled, fills whole blocks with its plane through their middle, and has solid blocks all round its opening; portals in mid-air are left alone. A track laid before the portal opened has to be placed again to connect, as at a nether portal. Trains only cross portals into another dimension (Create links dimensions at a portal), so a Create Teleporters pair within one dimension still has no train bridge. The opening now gives off light, as a nether portal does. Create Teleporters' own frame check takes IP's portal blocks in its opening as empty (it would otherwise switch its portal off and on every second); an opening whose portal another mod removes right after it got portal blocks, three times in a row, is left without them until the server stops.

## [2.5.1] - 2026-10-21

### Fixed
- With Figura and a scripted avatar, the world seen through a turned or resized portal was drawn upright and at its own size (a ship-mounted portal on a tilting ship, a portal linked to a frame of another size or orientation, a mirror), while walking through still turned and scaled the player. Figura's camera hook in `GameRenderer.renderLevel` builds the view matrix itself without calling the hook it wraps, so when it was outermost IP's camera transformation never ran. IP's hook now applies after other mods' (it is outermost and transforms the matrix they built), and if another mod still skips it, IP applies the transformation before frustum culling instead (logged once as `[IPCamera]`). Only rendering was affected.

## [2.5.0] - 2026-10-20

First public release since 2.4.15. Groups the test builds 2.4.16-2.4.25 (details in their entries below): Create shafts through portals, with any kinetic block at either end, stress shared as one network, motors on both sides acting as one network, and half shafts drawn so a shaft looks continuous through the portal (also at ship-mounted portals, and on Sable ships through their own ship-mounted portal); fluid pipes through ship-mounted portals; pipes at a portal with nothing on the far side no longer cut off at the plane, at once when placed and at ship-mounted portals too; ships joined by a swivel bearing resting half through a portal; and Create trains carrying a ship no longer vanishing on their next trip through a portal. Declared incompatible with Create Transferables, which carries shafts and pipes through portals itself.
