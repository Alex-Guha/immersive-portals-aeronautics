# Changelog: 2.6

## [2.6.12] - 2026-10-28

### Fixed
- Crouching to light a vanilla nether portal with flint and steel also works while flying: holding sneak in the air gave IP's portal, since the game never counts a flying player as crouching. The sneak key decides now, as for vanilla's sneak-click.

## [2.6.11] - 2026-10-28

### Fixed
- Interactive Foliage (with Sway): with a shader pack, a portal to another dimension in view no longer drops the game to a few fps. Interactive Foliage recompiled its shaders and rebuilt all its foliage for each portal view and again for the player's view, every frame. Its waving foliage now belongs to the player's world: other dimensions seen through portals show their plants still, instead of missing near the player's position, and crossing a portal hands the waving over to the new world. Sway updates its bends once a frame instead of once per view (`IPFoliageCompat`).

## [2.6.10] - 2026-10-28

### Fixed
- Create Aeronautics: with a shader pack, a laser beam between the camera and a portal no longer hides the portal's view behind it (the pixels behind the beam showed this side's world, as if the portal were not there). The beam now shows in front of the portal's view, drawn inside the view so it meets the beam's far part without a seam (`IPLaserIrisPortals`).

## [2.6.9] - 2026-10-27

### Fixed
- Create: Pipes 'n Physics: fluid in pipes on the far side of a portal no longer shows through the portal's view (looking into a nether portal showed the water in the pipes behind the far portal). The same goes for anything else other mods draw at the end of a terrain layer.

## [2.6.8] - 2026-10-27

### Added
- Create Aeronautics: handles on a ship straddling a portal can be held from the far side, on the part of the ship that sticks out there, and handles seen through a portal can be grabbed (right click, or holding use to catch one while falling past). A held handle stays held when the player or the ship goes through the portal: hanging from a ship as it flies through a portal carries the player through with it. Pulling a ship by its handle (sneaking) works across the portal too, and a player standing on the far half cannot pull the ship they stand on.

### Fixed
- Right-clicking a block through a portal or on the far half of a straddling ship no longer acts on whatever is at the same coordinates in the player's own world for mods that handle the click themselves (Create does this for handles, among others): it could fail, or use the wrong block, after a ship had been in the player's dimension before.

## [2.6.7] - 2026-10-27

### Fixed
- Euphoria Patches (Euphoria Patcher with Complementary): the game no longer drops to about 1 fps, with flickering portal views, whenever a portal to another dimension is in view, and walking through a portal no longer freezes the game for up to a second and a half. Euphoria rebuilt the whole shader pack, and every loaded chunk with it, each time the dimension being drawn changed: twice a frame with a portal in view, and on every crossing. Each dimension now keeps its own shader pipeline, built once from Euphoria's settings for that dimension.

## [2.6.6] - 2026-10-27

### Added
- Create: Pipes 'n Physics: pipes through nether portals and framed portals (Create Teleporters') work with its engine on. Before, a pipe into a portal was a dead end. The portal is a relay, the way Pipes 'n Physics treats docking connectors: fluid pushed in comes out on the far side and flows on there by its own physics (level or downhill, or a pump lifts it); pressure does not carry through, and fluid goes one way at a time. Frameless portals stay dead ends. Up to a bucket per pipe end that is between the two sides when the server stops is lost.

## [2.6.5] - 2026-10-27

### Fixed
- Create: a pump placed directly against a portal (no pipe between them) now pumps through it. The far side got no pressure, so the fluid stopped at the portal while the near pipes showed it flowing.

## [2.6.4] - 2026-10-24

First public release since 2.5.7. Groups 2.5.8-2.6.3 (details in their entries below) with this release's own fixes: Create trains through portals that lead back into the same dimension; a config screen of our own, so Cloth Config is no longer needed, and one entry in the Mods list; spacer dimensions (Void, Skyland, Chaos) back in the dimension stack; Voxy (through Roxy) LODs in portal views, with or without a shader pack; with a shader pack, portal views no longer show block entities, entities and particles from behind the far portal or leave out whole chunks' surfaces, and Create's shafts through portals look whole again; works alongside Infinite Dimensions (no crash or kick when it adds a dimension, no crash after a vanilla-style dimension change), Create: Pocket Sized (scaled ships collide at their size), Cobbleloots, Aeronautics Camera Sync 1.4.0 and GLASS Remastered (crash on start).

### Fixed
- With a shader pack (or Flywheel's backend off for any other reason): the turning shaft pieces inside a portal's blocks, which join a shaft to the portal and show it going through, were missing on both sides. They were drawn only through Flywheel, which every shader pack switches off; they are now drawn the way Create draws its own shafts then, and look the same as the shaft next to them.
- With a shader pack: the last sliver of a pipe stub (or anything else in the terrain ending in the middle of a nether portal) no longer shows on the portal's other face. Fixed without a pack in 1.22.4.

## [2.6.3] - 2026-10-24

### Fixed
- GLASS Remastered: the game no longer crashes on start with both installed. Both mods change the eye position used for the sky, and GLASS requires its change; IP now wraps the call instead of replacing it, so both apply (GLASS's projection camera outside portal views, IP's camera inside them).

## [2.6.2] - 2026-10-24

### Added
- Spacer dimensions for the dimension stack are back: Void, Skyland, Chaos and the fully bright Void and Skyland (upstream turned them off in its NeoForge port). Pick them in the dimension stack screen (`/portal dimension_stack`, or More > Dimension Stack when creating a world) or name them in a dedicated server's preset; a spacer is created when a stack uses it and is there again after every restart. Adding one the stack already has gives a new copy (Void 2, Void 3, ...), so a stack can hold several; how thick each one is comes from its top and bottom Y in the entry's Edit screen. Spacers are not written into `level.dat`, so the world still loads without the mod.

### Fixed
- With Astral Sorcery: looking down into the overworld through a dimension stack's floor (from a dimension stacked above it) showed black everywhere below the horizon while the player's eyes were below y 63. Astral Sorcery drew the overworld's sky inside that view, which shows no sky.

## [2.6.1] - 2026-10-24

### Fixed
- Aeronautics Camera Sync 1.4.0: looking through a portal while standing on a tilted ship no longer shows the far side from the wrong place (it swung tens of blocks as the ship rocked), and the camera's own tilt no longer eases towards level while a portal is in view. Camera Sync took each portal view for the player's own camera; the view through a portal now turns with the player's tilted view.

## [2.6.0] - 2026-10-24

### Added
- Voxy (through Roxy): portal views show the destination's Voxy LODs past its loaded terrain, limited to the portal, into other dimensions and the player's own alike, with or without a shader pack.

### Fixed
- Voxy (through Roxy): portals show their own view again instead of drawing the other side's world across the whole screen (Voxy switched off the stencil test that limits a portal view to the portal), and a portal view no longer paints the far dimension's LODs over the sky around the portal.
- Voxy with a shader pack that supports it: shaders no longer switch themselves off when Immersive Portals loads a second dimension. Voxy bound each dimension's renderer to the shader pack's current pipeline, which allows only one; each dimension now gets its own.
- Voxy: a crash (`ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException` in `MipGen`) when Voxy prepared block textures for two dimensions at once; its texture preparation shares one scratch buffer, so the dimensions now take turns.
- Voxy: a dimension's LODs no longer miss everything below y 0 (the whole surface of a superflat world) when its Voxy renderer was made while the player stood in a dimension starting at y 0, as after switching a shader pack off in the Nether.
- Voxy: `/voxy reload` (and switching Voxy off in its settings) no longer freezes the game; the Voxy renderers of the other dimensions Immersive Portals keeps loaded are shut down too.
- With a shader pack, portal views no longer leave out the surface of whole chunks (or swathes of them) until the player walks through: the pack's shadow pass inside a portal view overwrote the list of chunk faces the view then drew.
