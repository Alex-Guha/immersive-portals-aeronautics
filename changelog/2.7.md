# Changelog: 2.7

## [2.7.12] - 2026-10-31

### Added
- Ships tied by a Create Aeronautics spring cross a portal together, and the spring keeps working on the other side. A ship through first waits for the other for as long as the spring holds, so a pair can rest half through a portal. The spring is drawn through the portal, on both sides. A spring tied to the world, or whose other end is in another dimension, snaps as its ship leaves.
- Joining a server with an incompatible version now gives a clear message naming both versions, instead of failing later on packets that changed. `Tolerant Version Mismatch with Server` lets a player join anyway. Versions before this one are all refused, because they cannot report their real version.
- A warning at load when Sable, Create, Create Aeronautics, Distant Horizons, Sodium or Iris is a version we did not test.
- The log names any hook into another mod that stopped matching, with a line per mod of how many are alive (`/ip_aeronautics compat` prints the same).
- Operators are told on joining when ships cannot straddle portals here, and why.

### Fixed
- Half shafts, pipe stubs and belt looks show at large portals on tilted ships.
- Vanilla beacon beams are cut at portals in portal views.
- With a shader pack that ships `gbuffers_terrain_solid` or `_cutout`, terrain behind the far portal no longer shows in portal views.
- A core-profile shader pack is no longer lost to our clip plane patch: its terrain goes unclipped in portal views, with a warning in the log.
- An error in one part of the ship tick no longer stops the others (straddling above all), and it is logged properly.
- `/ip_aeronautics straddle off` releases ships that were let through a portal's frame.
- Worlds left no longer stay in memory through the ship render watchdog or the ship state on the server.
- Shafts through a large portal on a tilted ship no longer come apart. A big ship tilting mid-crossing keeps its session.
- Joining a server without the mod after one with it no longer sends it our movement packets.

### Removed
- Physics Mod's ocean handling, inherited from the fork. Physics Mod turns its ocean, snow and vine physics off by itself next to Immersive Portals; only skipping its draw in portal views stays.
- The experimental Iris renderer (a debug switch), three mixins for an old Flywheel, Distant Horizons' C2ME guard, which did nothing on 3.3 but ran on every config read, and empty files.

## [2.7.11] - 2026-10-31

### Fixed
- With Sundial Lite and Distant Horizons, the sky in portal views no longer turns black and LODs show there: a dimension stack's floor and ceiling always, other portals while their far side was still loading.
- With a shader pack, several portal views into the same dimension no longer blur each other: each gets its own render state, as views of the player's own world have since 2.7.2, up to three per dimension, with the views looked at directly first. This uses more video memory while they are in view.
- Chunks no longer vanish from portal views and come back over and over between 15 and 30 fps. Automatic performance adjustment now cuts portal loading that hard only at 15 fps or below, or with little free memory.

## [2.7.10] - 2026-10-31

### Added
- Portals on ships have a third mode between on and off: Limited. A nether portal on a ship breaks when its link moves too far from a normal one, so a ship can no longer carry a link across the world. Ship Portal Range sets how far, 64 overworld chunks by default. A block in the nether counts as 8, and height does not count. With both sides on ships the link is measured between them, so two ships flying together at 8:1 stay in range. Custom and datapack portals have no limit. New configs start Limited; existing configs keep what they had. The config option `shipMountedPortals` is now `shipPortalMode` (off, limited, unlimited) and is converted on the first start.

### Fixed
- Portals on ships: when a portal shatters with its other side not loaded, that side now breaks too, as soon as it loads. It used to stay behind: a fixed side stayed open and led nowhere, and a ship kept invisible portal blocks that stopped its frame from being lit again.
- A portal with both ends on ships no longer shatters when one of the ships has been unloaded for 30 seconds.
- A Create shaft driven through a portal stops when it loads and the portal is gone, as after a reload with the other side not loaded when the portal broke. It used to keep turning forever with nothing driving it. A portal that is still there keeps its shafts turning until the other side is back.

## [2.7.5] - 2026-10-30

### Added
- Both ends of a portal on ships (feature request #11): each side of a portal can be on a ship, on two ships (in one dimension or two) or both on the same ship, each side moving with its own ship. A nether portal lit on a ship gets its other side onto a second ship by assembling that side's frame; custom portals (portal wand, `/portal` commands) by assembling the blocks each side touches (both sides at once if one ship takes both), by `/ip_aeronautics shipportals mount` (it now mounts every side that is not on a ship yet and touches one), or by drawing both sides of a wand portal on ships. Disassembling either ship leaves its side in the world where its blocks land and the other side on its ship; removing either ship shatters the portal. Players, mobs, items and ships go through both ways, also resting half through while either ship moves (the physics engine moves the far half with both ships; natives rebuilt), and frameless ones get a rim at each end on its own ship. Create shafts, pipes and belts through such a portal join a ship's machines to the other ship's (or, on one ship, to its own), as they join a ship's to the world at a portal with one side on a ship.

### Fixed
- Create's moving parts (shafts, belts, cogs and the like drawn by Flywheel) on ships no longer go invisible after turning a shader pack off or on, F3+A, or Immersive Portals reloading a world's renderer, most visibly on a ship in another dimension seen through a portal (bugs.md #8: they stayed invisible until the ship was synced again, and a relog brought back only some). Whenever Flywheel starts over in a world, that world's ships are rebuilt once (meshes stay on screen until rebuilt), which gives their parts back to Flywheel. A section being built (ships and terrain) now hands its parts to Flywheel by its own world: Flywheel asked which world was being drawn at that moment, so a part of the world behind a portal built during the player's view (or one of the player's world built during a portal view) was drawn by neither Flywheel nor Create until its section was built again.
- A pipe through a portal with a side on a ship no longer shows a thin sliver of the ship's half in the portal view from the other side of the portal (most visible with both ends on ships). Portal views clip ships 1 cm short of the far portal, so the last centimetre of the pipe's part up to the plane showed; the ship carrying the far side of a nether-framed portal is now clipped exactly at its plane.

## [2.7.3] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- Portal view distance (client option, off by default; feature request #11): a portal view shows the far side only so many chunks past the portal and fades into the far world's fog there, so less is drawn behind portals and they cost less frame rate (`IPPortalViewDistance`). Terrain past it is not searched or drawn (Sodium's search distance is capped in that view, with or without a shader pack), Distant Horizons draws no LODs in such a view, since they would lie behind the fog, and portals, entities (name tags included) and particles wholly past it are not drawn, which for portals saves their whole views. With a shader pack the view ends in the pack's own border fog (Iris's `far` and `dhRenderDistance` follow the cut in that view); Complementary's border fog, which runs from the camera, is measured over a band just before the cut in such a view, so a distant portal stays clear up to the set distance past it as with other packs. Walking or flying up to a portal, the fog moves out to the full render distance and then, with Distant Horizons, out to the LOD distance, over the last 24 blocks or more (three seconds of travel at speed), so crossing shows no pop. Not for dimension stack floors and ceilings, mirrors or fuse-view portals.

## [2.7.2] - 2026-10-29

### Fixed
- With a shader pack, a portal into the world you are in no longer blurs the whole screen, leaves ghosts of the portal's view, or shows flickering noise on LODs. Iris keeps one pipeline per dimension, with state a pack carries from frame to frame (TAA history, the previous camera and matrices); the portal's view and yours drew through the same one, so each took the other's image as its last frame, and the portal's view was shaded with your camera's per-frame values. Portal views of your own world now draw through a second pipeline of their own (`IPIrisPortalViewPipelines`). The first such view after joining or loading a pack builds it: a short hitch, like the first look through a nether portal, and the GPU memory of one more dimension. Not with Voxy LODs bound to a pack that supports them (those views share as before).

## [2.7.1] - 2026-10-29

### Added
- Custom portals on ships: portals made with the portal wand or `/portal` commands can be mounted on a Sable ship, move with it, and lead to a fixed other side, in the same dimension or another one (feature request #9; `docs/completed/custom-mounts.md`). A ship that is bigger on the inside: a door on the hull leading to a room built elsewhere, whose own door looks out wherever the ship is.
  - Build the portal on a structure and assemble it: the portal comes along, like a lit nether portal. On a ship that is already assembled, `/ip_aeronautics shipportals mount` mounts the portal you look at (either of its sides) on the ship its edge touches; `unmount` leaves it where it is.
  - Draw it on a ship with the portal wand: corners clicked on a ship's blocks stay on the ship however it moves (the marks, the plane and the outline are drawn with it), and the side drawn there is mounted on it. The other side goes anywhere off ships.
  - A custom portal belongs to the blocks it touches: disassembling the ship leaves it in the world where those blocks land. Breaking blocks around it does nothing.
  - Where a nether portal on a ship shatters (the ship flying through another portal, the option switched off, the ship removed), a custom portal shatters too: both sides go, with the sound and particles.
  - One side per portal on a ship: a portal with both sides on the assembled blocks stays in the world, as before.
  - Ships, players and items go through both ways, also when both sides are in one dimension.
  - A frameless custom portal keeps its rim on a ship: objects half through it rest against the edge instead of sliding out of the side, on the ship's side (the rim moves with the ship) and on the fixed side.

### Fixed
- A frameless portal framed by a ship's blocks no longer gets a rim (the rim pushed the ship).
- An object resting half through a two-sided frameless portal was sometimes thrown out of it, depending on which of the portal's two sides got the rim. Both sides now build the same rim.

## [2.7.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- Create belts through portals: two belts meeting at a portal work and look like one belt (`IPCreatePortalBelts`, `IPCreatePortalBeltsClient`, `IPCreatePortalBeltLook`, `IPCreatePortalBeltRiders`).
  - A belt ending at a portal hands its items to whatever takes belt input at the same spot past it (another belt, a belt's side, a depot, a chute, a machine), as Create hands items from one belt to the next. Items run on to the portal's plane and carry on from the far plane without a jump, cut at the plane so they pass through like one item, and a full belt keeps its spacing. Before, items were thrown off the belt's end and piled up inside the far portal block.
  - One belt turning turns the belt across the portal, and the two share stress; two belts with their own power stay two belts.
  - Players, mobs and dropped items riding the belt are carried through the portal at the belt's height and onto the far belt.
  - A belt ending at a portal runs straight on into the portal block, scrolling with the belt. With no belt across it ends in the far portal block with its end loop; two belts across from each other that do not run as one (running into each other, or at different speeds) each end at the plane.
  - All of it also for a belt on a ship at the ship's own mounted portal, with the belt at the fixed side.

### Fixed
- Create belts: a rider was held just before any portal a belt runs into, and walking on there was as slow as through cobwebs (the belt took the portal for something in the way).
- Create at portals: only one thing goes through the space between the two sides of a portal at one spot, the highest of what faces the portal there on either side: a belt's end, then a shaft, then a pipe, then a track. It keeps its look and what it does through the portal; the other draws nothing in its portal block and passes nothing through, as if it faced a wall. Before, a sideways belt's pulley across the portal drew its shaft through the portal where the belt on this side runs its items, and a shaft and a pipe facing each other drew into each other (`IPCreatePortalEnds`).
- Shadows of items on Create belts, and entity shadows, behind the far side of a portal no longer show in the portal's view (back-bleed: their shaders now get IP's clip plane).
