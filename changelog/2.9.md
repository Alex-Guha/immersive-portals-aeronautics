# Changelog: 2.9

## [Unreleased Changes]

## [2.9.2] - 2026-11-13

### Fixed
- On a server, chunks with an elevator from Elevator Mod in them load on the client again. Before, they never appeared, and the log showed a NullPointerException in `IPCreatePortalEndScan.onBlockChanged` (#1).
- With Create: Pocket Sized installed, the game no longer crashes now and then on joining a world (`IllegalArgumentException` from `ClientWorldLoader.getClientWorlds` in Pocket Sized's `ScaleHandshake`).

## [2.9.1] - 2026-11-13

### Changed
- Nothing changes in game. Every push to the repository is now built and checked on GitHub, and runs the headless test suite (148 tests of ships, portals, trains and Create parts at portals, run on a test server), so a change that breaks one shows up before a jar is released.
- Building from source: the build stops when a physics library in `natives/` was built from older Rust source than the checkout's (`-PallowStaleNatives` packages it anyway), Create Teleporters and Create: Pipes 'n' Physics come from Modrinth's maven instead of local jars, the unit tests run again, and `scripts/check.sh` runs the static checks (`CONTRIBUTING.md`).

### Removed
- Fabric and JitPack build files left over from upstream.

## [2.9.0] - 2026-11-12

### Added
- Ships go through scaled portals with Create: Pocket Sized, and change size as they pass: a ship straddling a portal of scale k shows, collides and is cut past the plane at k times its size, and when it is through, Pocket Sized makes the whole ship that size where it is. Speed past the plane scales with it. The new size is held to the scales Pocket Sized allows players; a ship it cannot resize is pushed back before it enters, and the far half finds its own room on the far side. A portal whose `teleportChangesScale` is off takes ships across in one step at their size; ships tied to others by ropes or joints, and ships whose size is changing, are pushed back from portals that change size. Without Pocket Sized, scaled portals stay closed to ships. Ropes on such a ship keep their length, as when Pocket Sized resizes a ship directly (it scales no rope physics).
- The physics staff and ship handles work through scaled portals: a ship can be grabbed through one or by the far half of a ship straddling one, keeps being dragged or held when it or the player goes through, and follows the aim at the size it is seen. Before, such a grab did nothing and a crossing let go. Mirrors still let go.

### Fixed
- The portal blocks of a portal on a ship no longer catch clicks: they could be broken by accident and stood in the way of what was behind them (a seat through the portal), where portal blocks in the world never did.
- Blocks on the part of a ship that is through a portal (seats, levers, doors) can be used from anywhere they are seen: from right beside that part, and through the portal over all of it. Before, a click from within half a block of that part's outline did nothing, so through the portal only some spots worked and a player shrunk to half size standing on it could not use it at all.
- A player seated on a ship now changes size going through a portal that changes size, as on foot: riding the ship through it, sitting down on the part of a ship that is already through, and coming back. Before, a seated player kept their size on the other side.
- Ships no longer jolt as they come out of a portal: the moment a ship was handed over to the other side, it moved at half speed for two ticks and then caught up (with Create: Pocket Sized, it could also jump ahead and stop for a moment).
- Resizing a ship that carries a portal while another ship is halfway through it now cuts that ship at the portal's new size on screen, not the size it had when it started through.
- A ship resized at a portal no longer shows at its new size in the world it is leaving for a frame before it appears on the other side.
- A ship going through a portal that shrinks it, into a two-sided doorway such as a nether portal, no longer comes straight back out of the doorway's other side the moment it arrives.
- With Create: Pocket Sized installed, players and mobs no longer fall through the part of a ship that is through a portal (and no longer stand on thin air where the part past the portal was): Pocket Sized merges a ship's blocks into larger collision boxes, which the cut at the portal removed or kept whole.

### Changed
- Ships scaled by Create: Pocket Sized straddle portals like unscaled ones: the portal cut, entity and block collisions across the plane, ropes and the hand-off follow the ship's scale, and the ship arrives at the same scale. A ship resized while it straddles a portal keeps straddling it and is handed off once its size has settled; a ship whose size is changing as it reaches a portal crosses in one step.
- Ships scaled by Create: Pocket Sized keep their size when seen from another dimension: through a portal, and the far half of a straddling ship, which were drawn, picked and collided with at 1x. Pocket Sized's client kept a ship's scale only while the ship was in the player's own dimension and set every other ship back to 1x; it now keeps it for every dimension we show, scale changes also reach players who see the ship through a portal, and a client's request for a ship's scale is answered from the ship's own dimension.
- Ships scaled by Create: Pocket Sized are cut cleanly at a portal: the straddle cut, the cut of a ship seen through a portal, a portal mounted on a scaled ship and hot-air balloon overlays put the plane where it is, not where it would be at 1x (which left scaled ships looking see-through, with parts missing depending on the view). Block cracks on the far half are drawn at the ship's size.
- A portal mounted on a ship scaled by Create: Pocket Sized grows and shrinks with the ship and fills its frame. It is then a scaled portal (its two ends differ in size): players, items and ships going through change size as at any scaled portal (a ship straddles it and comes out at its size times the portal's scale). `shipportals status` gives each mounted portal's size and scale.
- Create shafts, belts and pipes keep working through a portal mounted on a ship scaled by Create: Pocket Sized, block for block in the ship's own blocks (they stopped, and a shaft drive through it was dropped, once the ship was scaled). Items on a belt through such a portal are drawn past the plane at the right place. Through other scaled portals Create parts still do not connect.
- Trains go through a portal on a ship scaled by Create: Pocket Sized and come out at the size of the tracks they arrive on (a train on a shrunk ship's tracks comes out full size in the world), as the portal takes one track block to one: the carriage half past the plane is drawn at the far side's size. A player riding the train through is resized by the portal's scale, as one walking through would be. Through a scaled portal between two unscaled sides trains do not go (a track block there faces half a block or two): its track does not link, and a pair already linked becomes two waiting ends while its portal's scale is not 1, linking again when it is back to 1.
- The shaft, pipe and belt pieces drawn in the portal blocks of a moving ship-mounted portal no longer vanish now and then (any ship, scaled or not): the client looked for the portal blocks by the ship's tick pose while the portal is drawn by its frame pose.

### Fixed
- Create contraptions (trains, moving parts on ships) no longer freeze or lose their scale while a portal view whose camera lands more than 256 blocks away is on screen (a view back through two scaled portals, a far portal in the same dimension): Flywheel moved its render origin to that view's camera and back for the main view, rebuilding every visual twice a frame and skipping their frame updates, so a train on a ship scaled by Create: Pocket Sized drew its body at 1x on its scaled wheels. Portal passes now keep Flywheel's render origin unless their camera is more than 8192 blocks from it.
