# Changelog: 2.10

## [Unreleased Changes]

### Fixed
- A portal that is gone no longer leaves its invisible portal blocks in the frame. When a portal was removed any other way than by breaking its frame (killed by a command or another mod, dropped as invalid, or lost from the world's save), its portal blocks stayed, and the frame could not be lit again until every one of them was broken by hand. Portal blocks that no portal holds now go by themselves: right away when their portal is removed, when a block next to them changes, and otherwise shortly after a player comes near (worlds that already have such blocks included). This also works for portals mounted on ships.
- The game no longer crashes on startup with Shadered installed. Both mods change how Minecraft reads its shader files, and only one mod at a time can change it the way this mod did; this mod now adds its change on top of Shadered's instead.
- Create elevators: assembling the blocks around one of an elevator's floors into a ship (or taking a ship apart) no longer sends the car on past every floor with its rope running out. Each floor remembered where the car was headed as a height, and kept the old height when the blocks moved; through a portal, the car then went looking for a floor as far below the right one as the blocks had moved. Floors now remember it relative to themselves.
- Create elevators through a floor portal built on the ground: riding a seat down, you are no longer thrown off the seat just before the portal and dropped onto the portal. Past the portal the car moves on through the ground behind it, which you never see, and Create threw off anyone whose seat ran into a solid block there before they reached the other side.
- Create elevators through a floor portal near the bottom of the world: a car parked further down than the world's floor (past the portal) no longer jumps up to the world's floor when the world is loaded and goes back down. While it moved, the car could vanish for you, leaving the parts of it past the portals stuck in the air and the rope moving alone until you rejoined.
- Map mods built on Surveyor Map Framework (Hoofprint, for example) now map the world: nothing was ever recorded. This mod sends the world to players itself and skipped Minecraft's own step for each piece of it, which Surveyor and other mods add to; that step now runs for every piece this mod sends, and parts of another dimension seen through a portal are mapped in that dimension. NeoForge's extra light data for blocks that light up through it now also comes with each piece, as without this mod.

## [2.10.1] - 2026-11-23

### Added
- Create shafts, pipes and tracks through a portal can be built on from the other side: when a shaft, pipe or track goes into a portal and nothing continues it on the other side, the piece of it sticking out of the portal there can now be pointed at like a block. Its outline shows, a shaft, pipe or track placed against its end goes where it would continue (and then joins it through the portal as usual), and middle-click in creative picks the part. With a shaft in hand, clicking any face of a half shaft coming out of a portal places the shaft where it continues, with Create's preview of where it goes, as on any shaft. A shaft placed next to a portal also turns to line up with a shaft on the portal's other side, as it does next to a shaft. Hitting the piece does not break the portal.
- Create elevators through several floor portals in a row: past a floor portal, the shaft keeps going down through the next floor portal below it on the other side, and so on (up to eight portals), for example from the Overworld through a portal into the End and from there through another portal into the Nether. Redstone contacts in every part of the shaft become floors of the same elevator, numbered from the bottom up across all of them; call the car from any of them and ride it all the way, seated riders included. Each portal must meet the same conditions as the first (the car fits inside its opening, not scaled, turned by quarter turns about the vertical at most), and a portal must be at least the car's height below the one above it; below a portal that does not, the car passes behind it as before.

### Changed
- The mod is now called Immersive Portals Revamped (before: Immersive Portals: Aeronautics), since it does much more than Aeronautics compat. Nothing else changes: its mod id stays `ip_aeronautics`, so worlds, configs, the `/ip_aeronautics` commands and addons built on it keep working, and the jar keeps its name.
- The mod's issue link (in NeoForge's mod list and crash reports) and the links in its chat messages now lead to this mod's GitHub issues and Modrinth page instead of the original Immersive Portals'.

### Fixed
- With a shader pack and Distant Horizons, portals seen through other portals no longer show a black sky and flat grey shapes where Distant Horizons' simplified terrain was drawn right up to the camera (looking up through a floor portal, for example). Simple Nested Portal Views now leaves out that simplified terrain wherever normal terrain is drawn, as shader packs do.
- With a shader pack and Colorwheel, Create contraptions and other moving Create parts are no longer missing in portals seen through other portals (an elevator car past its second portal, for example), and such views no longer show a grey patch and a black sky. Simple Nested Portal Views is not used while Colorwheel draws Create's parts: those views are drawn with your shader pack, as with the option off.
- Create elevators through floor portals with a shader pack and without Colorwheel: the pulley's belt no longer has a gap just above the portal and a piece shifted sideways above that gap.
- Create pipes, shafts and belts facing each other through a portal mounted on a ship no longer draw on top of each other. Only one part goes through a portal at a spot, belts before shafts before pipes; at a ship-mounted portal the pipe still drew its piece into the portal next to the shaft's, and a shaft into a belt's, as if both went through.
- Create belts through a portal without a shader pack: when the belt on the other side of the portal is broken (or placed), the remaining belt now runs on through the portal right away, as it should. When it was in the world seen through the portal rather than the one you stood in, it kept its old end cap until it was redrawn for another reason, next to the new end loop on the far side.
- Create elevators on a ship turned on its side: you no longer slide along the shaft, toward one end of the car, while standing in a car stopped at a floor. A stopped car kept the motion of its last step and carried whoever stood in it along; upright that only pushed along the vertical, where Create ignores it, but on a ship on its side it pushed sideways.
- Camera mods that move the third-person camera (Punchy! Moves' shoulder camera, AFK and cinematic camera mods) no longer break portal views. Depending on load order, such a mod moved the camera of each portal view back to the player's own position, so the portal (or, with the camera behind a portal, the whole screen) showed the other dimension from the wrong place, often at this dimension's coordinates. With Punchy! Moves' shoulder camera, your own player also no longer disappears from the view through a portal.

## [2.10.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added
- Create elevators through floor portals: an elevator whose shaft runs down through a portal in the floor keeps going on the other side. Redstone contacts in the shaft on both sides become floors of the same elevator when it starts, as Create does on the pulley's side: call the car from either side, and pick any floor on the car's controls from either side; floors are numbered from the bottom up across both sides. While the car is partly through, each side shows its own part of it and of the pulley's belt, and you can stand or sit in it and ride it through the portal: seated riders change sides at the portal plane. Portals on ships work too: an elevator built on a ship runs through a portal on that ship, and an elevator runs through a portal whose other side is on a ship, both while the ships fly; an elevator fixed in the world does not run through a portal that a ship carries. The whole car must fit inside the portal's opening, and the portal must not be scaled. A portal that turns things by a quarter, half or three-quarter turn about the vertical (a ship's portal disassembled facing another way turns its end with it) turns the car on the other side to match. With any other portal the car passes behind it as before. Chests and other storage on the car hold the same items from either side. While the car is partly through, the pulley refuses to stop it, and Create Aeronautics' physics assembler refuses to assemble or disassemble a ship holding the pulley (goggles say why): bring the car to a floor on the pulley's side first.

### Changed
- Placing a Portal Helper no longer posts the "Portal Helper is now deprecated" message in chat. The Portal Helper works as before; its tooltip still says how to light it.

### Fixed
- Create pulleys and elevators on ships no longer throw a player standing on them about 190 blocks into the air as they start to move. Create's guard against sinking into a moving contraption measured the contraption in the ship's own coordinates and the player in the world's.
- Create contraptions moved by a block (rope pulleys, elevators, pistons) no longer vanish when they move through a portal. The contraption used to be carried to the other side, where it found no pulley or piston and deleted itself with all its blocks, leaving its pulley or piston stuck. It now stays in its own dimension. Pulleys and pistons stop at a portal's blocks as they would at a solid block, and their blocks are placed on the near side, so the portal no longer breaks. Without portal blocks, as with dimension stacks and portals made with commands, the contraption passes behind the portal plane. It is no longer drawn a second time on the portal's other side, where it isn't. A Create contraption never places its blocks over a portal's blocks: a block that would have gone there drops as an item.
- A ship straddling two portals into the same dimension at once (two portals a few blocks apart, the ship across both) is now solid at both ends in that dimension: players and mobs stand on, and bump into, the part through either portal. Before, only one end was solid there and you could walk or fly through the other.
- The part of a straddling ship through a portal is now solid for players and mobs on the other side when the ship rests on something at the portal's plane (lying on the ground at a floor portal, or pressed against a frame). Before, a sliver of the ship just past the plane let you fall or fly straight through that part.
- Standing or walking on the part of a straddling ship through a portal no longer makes the view dip for a moment now and then, as if you crouched or started crawling for a frame. The server let your player sink through that part between your client's position updates and then had you crouch or crawl under it.
- A ship left through a portal when you quit (or when its area unloads) is through the same portal when it loads again. Before, it came back with no part through any portal until it moved, and then took whichever side of the portal it moved into: a ship across two nether portals that face each other could take the wrong side of one, fall through the ground and be lost. It also waits where it was saved until the other side of its portals has loaded: a ship resting partly on its part through a portal no longer drops, flips or is thrown out of the world as you join. And when the far side of its portal is on a ship that loads after it, its part through the portal follows that ship again when it moves: it lagged behind, and moving the ship quickly dropped it out of the portal.
- A rare crash when joining a world ("Exception in world tick" in the dimension stack's check for fluid falling through a stack floor), when a client tick came before the portals' other worlds were set up.
- Ship-mounted portals: after disassembling a ship carrying a portal, coming through the portal's other side no longer sends you straight back (most noticeable with a floor portal: coming up from the Nether dropped you back down, sometimes over and over). Your game kept the other side where the ship had last been, a fraction of a block off. The same happened on the server when the other side was not loaded at the time (for example soon after loading the world); a portal disassembled somewhere else then broke shortly afterwards. The other side now moves to match as soon as it loads, also after quitting and reloading in between. Portals already affected stay as they are: break and relight them.
- Create: Pocket Sized: packing a ship with a portal into a case (box, bottle, display plate) now breaks the portal at both sides as it is packed, with the usual sound and particles; the case keeps the frame, so the portal can be lit again once the ship is unpacked. Before, the other side stayed open onto where the ship had been, and unpacking broke it. This works with Pocket 0.24 and 0.32; with 0.32 it also holds for the ships hot-glued to it that go into the case with it.
- Create: Pocket Sized 0.32 hats: a worn hat and a build on a hat stand are no longer taken for ships at portals. A worn hat does not go through a portal or hang across one on its own (Pocket brings it along with you). A portal cannot be lit on a hat or a hat stand build (or a ship placed on one), and putting a ship with a portal on a hat breaks the portal, as packing it into a case does: Pocket keeps these builds outside the world, where a portal cannot follow them.
- Create: Pocket Sized 0.32 hats: a ship put on a hat or a hat stand no longer vanishes, and hat builds and held cases show all their blocks. Pocket's copies of these builds on your screen were drawn empty. A worn hat also shows on a player seen through a portal (yourself in third person included).
- Create: Pocket Sized: a ship packed into a case and unpacked again can hold a portal again. Before, any portal lit on it broke a tick later until the world was reloaded.
- Create: Pocket Sized: a portal near a ship being packed no longer goes into the case with it.
- A ship no longer sheds pieces that are still attached when its portal breaks (out of range, crossing another portal) or when several of its blocks go at once (an explosion). Sable's check for ships that came apart could split off attached blocks when the block it measures the ship from was among the removed ones, and then keep a wrong block count, which could later turn the whole ship into a new one. Ships that really come apart still split as before.
- Pehkui: walking or falling through a portal that changes size no longer leaves you briefly too slow (going smaller) or too fast (going bigger). Your speed now follows your new size from the first step; before, the portal's scaling was applied twice for about a quarter of a second. The same holds for mobs.
