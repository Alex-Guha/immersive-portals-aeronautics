# Changelog: 2.9

## [Unreleased Changes]

### Changed
- Placing a Portal Helper no longer posts the "Portal Helper is now deprecated" message in chat. The Portal Helper works as before; its tooltip still says how to light it.

### Fixed
- Ship-mounted portals: after disassembling a ship carrying a portal, coming through the portal's other side no longer sends you straight back (most noticeable with a floor portal: coming up from the Nether dropped you back down, sometimes over and over). Your game kept the other side where the ship had last been, a fraction of a block off. The same happened on the server when the other side was not loaded at the time (for example soon after loading the world); a portal disassembled somewhere else then broke shortly afterwards. The other side now moves to match as soon as it loads, also after quitting and reloading in between. Portals already affected stay as they are: break and relight them.
- Create: Pocket Sized: packing a ship with a portal into a case (box, bottle, display plate) now breaks the portal at both sides as it is packed, with the usual sound and particles; the case keeps the frame, so the portal can be lit again once the ship is unpacked. Before, the other side stayed open onto where the ship had been, and unpacking broke it.
- Create: Pocket Sized: a ship packed into a case and unpacked again can hold a portal again. Before, any portal lit on it broke a tick later until the world was reloaded.
- Create: Pocket Sized: a portal near a ship being packed no longer goes into the case with it.
- A ship no longer sheds pieces that are still attached when its portal breaks (out of range, crossing another portal) or when several of its blocks go at once (an explosion). Sable's check for ships that came apart could split off attached blocks when the block it measures the ship from was among the removed ones, and then keep a wrong block count, which could later turn the whole ship into a new one. Ships that really come apart still split as before.
- Pehkui: walking or falling through a portal that changes size no longer leaves you briefly too slow (going smaller) or too fast (going bigger). Your speed now follows your new size from the first step; before, the portal's scaling was applied twice for about a quarter of a second. The same holds for mobs.

## [2.9.29] - 2026-11-20

### Fixed
- F3+U: with a frustum captured in front of a portal, the portal leading back on the far side (which the captured camera only sees from behind) is no longer drawn inside that portal's view. It still shows once you have walked through and turned around to face it.
- F3+U: after walking through a portal with a frustum captured, the portal on the back of the far side's frame (facing the captured camera, which looks through that frame) is no longer drawn.

## [2.9.28] - 2026-11-20

### Changed
- F3+U with Distant Horizons: LODs now follow the captured frustum through portals as chunks do. After walking through a portal, only the LODs inside that portal's opening as seen from the captured spot stay drawn, instead of every LOD in view; LODs a portal hides from the captured spot are left out too. With a Distant Horizons version that honours F3+U itself, LODs are fully frozen; with others, they still follow the live view as well.
- F3+U: the frozen frustum's outline no longer has a far end. Its far face sat at the edge of your view distance and flickered over Distant Horizons' LODs and whatever lay behind it; the sides now run on until your view ends. Culling is unchanged.

## [2.9.27] - 2026-11-20

### Changed
- With a shader pack and Simple Nested Portal Views on, the Portal Frame Rate Target first draws small portals you look at directly with the same plain look as nested ones (under 2% of the screen, later under 10%), before it hides any portal view behind the nether portal texture. A plain view costs a fraction of a shaded one, so the frame rate recovers with smaller changes, and fewer portals turn purple.

## [2.9.26] - 2026-11-20

### Fixed
- A ship falling or flying fast through portals placed close together (such as a loop of stacked portals between two dimensions) no longer stops dead on the ground behind the next portal. It keeps speeding up to full speed, even when one tick takes it through more than one portal.
- A ship coming fast out of a portal into another dimension is no longer drawn for a moment whole in front of the portal it came out of (above it, for a ship falling out of a ceiling portal).

## [2.9.25] - 2026-11-20

### Fixed
- EntityCulling with a shader pack: since 2.9.23, a portal view's shadows kept EntityCulling from hiding mobs between the portal and the sun for a moment, everywhere, which cost frame rate in your own view too.

## [2.9.24] - 2026-11-20

### Performance
- Deciding which portals are hidden behind something no longer makes the game wait for the graphics card several times a frame. With several portals about, most of them out of sight, that wait took a large share of every frame, most with a shader pack. A portal that comes out from behind a wall may now show its view one frame late. Inherited from Immersive Portals.

## [2.9.23] - 2026-11-20

### Fixed
- EntityCulling: mobs, players and other entities seen through a portal into the same dimension no longer vanish and reappear, and keep moving and animating normally. EntityCulling hid whatever the player could not see directly, from where the player stands; block entities such as chests are kept too. Views into other dimensions no longer miss entities EntityCulling hid while the player was last there.

## [2.9.22] - 2026-11-20

### Added
- Client option Portal View Shadows (Full by default): with a shader pack, what casts sun and moon shadows in the world seen through a portal. Terrain Only leaves out mobs, players, Create contraptions and block entities; Off casts none, so the view is lit evenly. Either costs much less with several portals in view. Your own view keeps its shadows. The Portal Performance Preset sets it: Off for Low, Terrain Only for Medium, Full for High and Unlimited.

## [2.9.21] - 2026-11-20

### Performance
- With a shader pack, a portal view draws into its shadow map only what can cast a shadow onto what its portal shows, instead of everything that could shade the whole screen from the far side. Entities (mobs, players, Create trains and contraptions) and terrain around the far portal cost far less in every portal view, most with portals small on screen. Shadows seen through the portal look the same.

## [2.9.20] - 2026-11-20

### Fixed
- A ship crossing back and forth between two portals into different dimensions several times a second (such as one falling through a portal loop) no longer crashes the game with a stack overflow.

## [2.9.13] - 2026-11-20

### Added
- Portal Performance Preset (Immersive Portals config, performance; Custom by default). Low, Medium, High and Unlimited set Max Portal Rendering Recursion Layer, Portal Draw Distance, View Distance Through Portals, Portal Frame Rate Target and Simple Nested Portal Views together, and grey them out; Custom lets you set them yourself: it keeps your values while you try other presets on the screen, and starts from the defaults when the screen was opened on another preset. Low: 1, 4 chunks, 4 chunks, 120, on. Medium: 2, 6, 8, 90, on. High: 5, render distance, render distance, 60, off. Unlimited: 10, 64, 32, off, off.

### Changed
- The Immersive Portals config screen shows its hover texts below the cursor and wider, so long ones are no longer cut off at the top of the screen.

### Removed
- Reduced Portal Rendering (Immersive Portals config, performance): Portal Draw Distance and View Distance Through Portals do what it did, and can be tuned. A config that had it on gets Portal Draw Distance 1 and View Distance Through Portals 4 (or keeps lower values of its own).
- Lag Attack Proof: its safety net for rooms full of mirrors or portals is now the last step of Portal Frame Rate Target. With more than 10 portal views and every one of the last 3 seconds under 8 fps, only portals within 16 blocks are drawn and none seen through other portals, until no second of the last 20 was at 15 fps or under. Portal Frame Rate Target 0 turns it off too.
- Enable Client Performance Adjustment. Your frame rate no longer makes the server load less through portals, and no longer halves how far a portal view draws: under 50 fps (normal with a shader pack) portals more than 3 chunks away could be drawn with nothing loaded behind them. With little free memory (under 800 MB on average) it still loads less, as before.

## [2.9.10] - 2026-11-20

### Added
- Portal Frame Rate Target (Immersive Portals config, performance; 60 by default, 0 turns it off). Every portal view draws the world again, and with a shader pack runs the whole pack again, so portals seen through portals add up fast. While the frame rate with portals in view stays under the target, the least visible views show the nether portal texture instead until it recovers: small portals seen through other portals first, then all of them, then very small distant portals. Views come back one step at a time; F3 shows the current step. Changing the target starts over with every view. Short hitches (crossing a portal, loading chunks) and the first seconds in a world do not count.
- Simple Nested Portal Views (Immersive Portals config, performance; on by default). With a shader pack, portals seen through other portals are drawn with a plain vanilla look instead of the pack's, which costs a fraction of what the pack does there: about 10% more frame rate with one such portal in view on a fast machine, more on a slower one. The portals you look at directly keep the pack.

### Performance
- With a shader pack, Immersive Portals' buffers for portal views are no longer prepared and cleared every frame when no portal view is drawn, the clears are gone in frames that draw them (four full-screen buffers each frame), and the graphics card vendor and depth formats are no longer read back from the driver every frame or every view, which made the CPU wait for the graphics driver.
- With a shader pack, a portal hidden behind a wall no longer costs full-screen copies every frame, a portal view with no portal inside it is copied once instead of twice, and each portal view skips one redundant full-screen stencil copy. A portal that comes into view is drawn from its first frame (it used to appear a frame late).

### Fixed
- With a shader pack, the depth-stencil format of portal view buffers follows the main framebuffer's real format again; from the second frame on it had gone back to a guess by graphics card vendor.

### Changed
- A portal past Max Portal Rendering Recursion Layer shows the nether portal texture instead of black.
- With a shader pack, a very small distant portal whose view the Portal Frame Rate Target drops shows the nether portal texture too; it had shown the terrain behind it when no other portal was drawn.
- Max Portal Rendering Recursion Layer is 3 instead of 5 in new configs. Existing configs keep their value.
- The hover text of Reduced Portal Rendering (it had none), Lag Attack Proof and Enable Client Performance Adjustment says what each does and when it acts.

## [2.9.6] - 2026-11-19

### Added
- Water, lava and other fluids falling into a dimension stack's floor no longer end flat in the sky of the dimension below. Only drawn: no fluid crosses into the dimension below.

## [2.9.0] - 2026-11-12

### Added
- Ships go through scaled portals with Create: Pocket Sized, and change size as they pass: a ship straddling a portal of scale k shows, collides and is cut past the plane at k times its size, and when it is through, Pocket Sized makes the whole ship that size where it is. Speed past the plane scales with it. The new size is held to the scales Pocket Sized allows players; a ship it cannot resize is pushed back before it enters, and the far half finds its own room on the far side. A portal whose `teleportChangesScale` is off takes ships across in one step at their size; ships tied to others by ropes or joints, and ships whose size is changing, are pushed back from portals that change size. Without Pocket Sized, scaled portals stay closed to ships. Ropes on such a ship keep their length, as when Pocket Sized resizes a ship directly (it scales no rope physics).
- The physics staff and ship handles work through scaled portals: a ship can be grabbed through one or by the far half of a ship straddling one, keeps being dragged or held when it or the player goes through, and follows the aim at the size it is seen. Before, such a grab did nothing and a crossing let go. Mirrors still let go.

### Fixed
- The portal blocks of a portal on a ship no longer catch clicks: they could be broken by accident and stood in the way of what was behind them (a seat through the portal), where portal blocks in the world never did.
- Blocks on the part of a ship that is through a portal (seats, levers, doors) can be used from anywhere they are seen: from right beside that part, and through the portal over all of it. Before, a click from within half a block of that part's outline did nothing, so through the portal only some spots worked and a player shrunk to half size standing on it could not use it at all.
- A player seated on a ship now changes size going through a portal that changes size, as on foot: riding the ship through it, sitting down on the part of a ship that is already through, and coming back. Before, a seated player kept their size on the other side.
- Ships no longer jolt as they come out of a portal: the moment a ship was handed over to the other side, it moved at half speed for two ticks and then caught up (with Create: Pocket Sized, it could also jump ahead and stop for a moment).
- Resizing a ship that carries a portal while another ship is halfway through it now cuts that ship at the portal's new size on screen, not the size it had when it started through.
- A ship resized at a portal no longer shows at its new size in the world it is leaving for a frame before it appears on the other side.
- A ship going through a portal that shrinks it, into a two-sided doorway such as a nether portal, no longer comes straight back out of the doorway's other side the moment it arrives.
- With Create: Pocket Sized installed, players and mobs no longer fall through the part of a ship that is through a portal (and no longer stand on thin air where the part past the portal was): Pocket Sized merges a ship's blocks into larger collision boxes, which the cut at the portal removed or kept whole.

### Changed
- Ships scaled by Create: Pocket Sized straddle portals like unscaled ones: the portal cut, entity and block collisions across the plane, ropes and the hand-off follow the ship's scale, and the ship arrives at the same scale. A ship resized while it straddles a portal keeps straddling it and is handed off once its size has settled; a ship whose size is changing as it reaches a portal crosses in one step.
- Ships scaled by Create: Pocket Sized keep their size when seen from another dimension: through a portal, and the far half of a straddling ship, which were drawn, picked and collided with at 1x. Pocket Sized's client kept a ship's scale only while the ship was in the player's own dimension and set every other ship back to 1x; it now keeps it for every dimension we show, scale changes also reach players who see the ship through a portal, and a client's request for a ship's scale is answered from the ship's own dimension.
- Ships scaled by Create: Pocket Sized are cut cleanly at a portal: the straddle cut, the cut of a ship seen through a portal, a portal mounted on a scaled ship and hot-air balloon overlays put the plane where it is, not where it would be at 1x (which left scaled ships looking see-through, with parts missing depending on the view). Block cracks on the far half are drawn at the ship's size.
- A portal mounted on a ship scaled by Create: Pocket Sized grows and shrinks with the ship and fills its frame. It is then a scaled portal (its two ends differ in size): players, items and ships going through change size as at any scaled portal (a ship straddles it and comes out at its size times the portal's scale). `shipportals status` gives each mounted portal's size and scale.
- Create shafts, belts and pipes keep working through a portal mounted on a ship scaled by Create: Pocket Sized, block for block in the ship's own blocks (they stopped, and a shaft drive through it was dropped, once the ship was scaled). Items on a belt through such a portal are drawn past the plane at the right place. Through other scaled portals Create parts still do not connect.
- Trains go through a portal on a ship scaled by Create: Pocket Sized and come out at the size of the tracks they arrive on (a train on a shrunk ship's tracks comes out full size in the world), as the portal takes one track block to one: the carriage half past the plane is drawn at the far side's size. A player riding the train through is resized by the portal's scale, as one walking through would be. Through a scaled portal between two unscaled sides trains do not go (a track block there faces half a block or two): its track does not link, and a pair already linked becomes two waiting ends while its portal's scale is not 1, linking again when it is back to 1.
- The shaft, pipe and belt pieces drawn in the portal blocks of a moving ship-mounted portal no longer vanish now and then (any ship, scaled or not): the client looked for the portal blocks by the ship's tick pose while the portal is drawn by its frame pose.

### Fixed
- Create contraptions (trains, moving parts on ships) no longer freeze or lose their scale while a portal view whose camera lands more than 256 blocks away is on screen (a view back through two scaled portals, a far portal in the same dimension): Flywheel moved its render origin to that view's camera and back for the main view, rebuilding every visual twice a frame and skipping their frame updates, so a train on a ship scaled by Create: Pocket Sized drew its body at 1x on its scaled wheels. Portal passes now keep Flywheel's render origin unless their camera is more than 8192 blocks from it.
