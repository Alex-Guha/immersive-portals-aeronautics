# Changelog: 2.2

## [2.2.4] - 2026-10-15

First public release since 2.1.0. Adds portals mounted on ships (2.2.0, on by default since this release) and groups the fixes of the test builds 2.2.1-2.2.3 (details in their entries below): the crash with Vista and Separate Sable Render Distance, terrain vanishing near portals with Jaden's Nether Expansion (Lodestone post-processing), and the fixes from the first in-game test of ship-mounted portals.

### Changed
- Ship-mounted portals (`shipMountedPortals`) are now on by default, as intended.

## [2.2.3] - 2026-10-15

### Fixed
- Loading the mod alongside Vista or Separate Sable Render Distance crashed the server (in singleplayer: on loading a world). IP replaced vanilla's per-player entity tracking update outright, and these mods' changes inside it found nothing to attach to. IP now skips that method instead of replacing it, so other mods' changes to it still apply. Vista's feeds from cameras outside the player's loaded area do not work yet (feature-requests.md).

## [2.2.2] - 2026-10-15

### Fixed
- With Jaden's Nether Expansion, the player's own terrain vanished or showed only in parts whenever a portal was on screen in the Nether (and for a few seconds after leaving it): its heat haze, a Lodestone post-processing effect, also ran inside portal views, and its full-screen pass wiped the whole frame outside the portal. Lodestone's post-processing effects (any mod using them) now run in the main view only, where they cover the whole screen, portal included.

## [2.2.1] - 2026-10-15

Fixes from the first in-game test of ship-mounted portals (2.2.0).

### Fixed
- A portal lit on a ship that had been through a portal before shattered at once ("its ship was removed"): a ship handed off to another dimension counted as destroyed for the rest of the session.
- Assembling a lit portal with Create Aeronautics' physics assembler shattered it ("only part of its frame"): Create leaves blocks without collision, such as portal blocks, out of what it moves. A portal is now mounted when its whole frame is assembled, and its portal blocks are put back on the ship.
- A ship-mounted portal broke when its ship was far away and unloaded while its portal was loaded again (seen through its other side): its frame was read as missing. Its frame is now only checked while its ship is loaded.

### Added
- Test hook `/ip_aeronautics shipportals debug assemble-solid <from> <to>` (assembles a box the way Create Aeronautics' assemblers select blocks).

## [2.2.0] - 2026-10-15

Portals mounted on ships (`docs/completed/ship-mounted-portals.md`, phases 0-4). Off by default.

### Added
- Config option `shipMountedPortals` in `immersive_portals.json` (off by default; `/ip_aeronautics shipportals on|off|status`, `-Dip_aeronautics.shipPortals=true|false`). With it on:
  - a nether portal can be lit on a ship: it moves with the ship, and its other side is a fixed portal in the other dimension, found or generated from where the ship was when it was lit;
  - a lit portal assembled into a ship (whole frame) is mounted on the ship instead of breaking; if its other side is already on a ship, both sides shatter; a frame assembled only in part shatters;
  - disassembling the ship turns its portal back into a normal portal where its frame lands;
  - players, mobs and items go through it both ways, with the ship's motion taken into account;
  - ships and physics objects go through it like through any other portal: they straddle it in both directions (into the ship's portal, and out of the fixed side onto or under the ship), rest half through, and are handed off with the ship's motion taken into account; the cut and the far half follow the ship as it is drawn. A ship never goes through its own portals;
  - a ship carrying portals that goes through any portal shatters them first; so do a ship being removed or destroyed, the frame being broken, and switching the option off.
- Physics: an image through a moving portal moves with the portal (natives: anchored images and clip regions, and the portal's frame velocity in contacts). Physics without moving portals is unchanged to the last digit.
- Test hooks `/ip_aeronautics shipportals debug light <pos>` (fire on the ship block at a world position) and `debug disassemble <pos> <goal> <quarterTurns>` (Create Aeronautics' disassembly); `shipportals status` lists the mounted portals.

### Fixed
- Lighting a nether portal frame on a ship no longer generates a portal frame millions of blocks away in the other dimension (IP read the ship's internal plot coordinates; the new pair broke at once). With the option off the frame now stays unlit.
- "Conflicting animation" was logged every tick for animated portals whose other side is in a dimension without players.
