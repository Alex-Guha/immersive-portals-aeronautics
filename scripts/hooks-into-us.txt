# Other mods' hooks into our classes, which scripts/check.sh checks against our freshly built jar: a renamed or removed
# member crashes the game for their players (required mixins) or quietly breaks their feature (reflection). Before
# changing a member named here, keep its old form or tell the other mod's author.
#
# One line per mod, "mod <Modrinth slug> <version id> <version> <mixin config> <mixin package folder>": check.sh
# downloads that release, decompiles its mixins with Vineflower and checks the ones into our packages. The weekly
# dependency check (.github/workflows/dependencies.yml) reports newer releases: bump the line, and update the reflected
# members below from the new jar.
